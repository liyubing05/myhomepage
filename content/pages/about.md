# Yubing Li &nbsp; 李玉冰

I am a Professor (研究员) and Doctoral Supervisor (博导) at the [Institute of Acoustics](http://www.ioa.ac.cn), [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://www.cas.cn). My research focuses on **medical ultrasound**, **computational imaging**, **signal processing**, and **artificial intelligence** for acoustic applications.

## Research Areas

My group works on high-resolution parameter imaging in medical ultrasound, developing innovative imaging techniques based on **Full Waveform Inversion (FWI)**. Our main directions include:

1. **Programmable ultrasound systems** — advanced acquisition techniques and GPU-based computing hardware
2. **FWI-based ultrasound imaging** — theory and methods for high-resolution reconstruction
3. **Ultrasound computed tomography** — clinical translation of quantitative tissue imaging
4. **AI-assisted high-performance computing** — next-generation modeling and inversion methods

Computational imaging and AI are powerful tools for obtaining high-resolution quantitative medical images, though challenges remain in algorithmic complexity and data interpretation. Our team focuses on advancing both methodology and clinical translation in ultrasound imaging.
