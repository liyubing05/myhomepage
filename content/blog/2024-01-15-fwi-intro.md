# Getting Started with Full Waveform Inversion

Full Waveform Inversion (FWI) is a powerful imaging technique that uses the full recorded wavefield to reconstruct high-resolution subsurface models.

## What is FWI?

FWI iteratively minimizes the misfit between observed and synthetic seismograms...

## Key Challenges

1. **Cycle skipping**: When the initial model is too far from the true model
2. **Computational cost**: Each iteration requires solving the wave equation multiple times
3. **Ill-posedness**: The inverse problem is highly nonlinear
